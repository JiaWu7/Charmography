 ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small objects.
Personal meaning.

Charmography explores how ordinary plush bag charms gain meaning through relationships, memories and everyday life.

The connecting o takes the form of a charm’s ring: a small piece that holds things together. Pull it away and the letters are released.
